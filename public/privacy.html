Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The Stillroom (“we”, “us”) is a social network for working professionals, run from India. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use thestillroom.site, why, and the choices you have. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

Account details

  • Your email address and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them).
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email and profile photo from your Google account.

Your profile

  • Name, city, role or job title, bio and profile photo — whatever you choose to add.

What you post and do

  • Forum threads, ideas, city chat messages, events you host or join, connections, and people you block.
  • Direct messages with your connections.
  • Your Clarity check-ins and steps.
  • When you were last active (used to show an “online” indicator).

Usage data

  • We use Google Analytics (through Firebase) to understand how people find and use the site: pages and screens viewed, how you arrived (for example, an invite link), approximate country and city, device and browser.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile to other members.
  • To help you find people and events in your city.
  • To deliver messages and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: preventing spam and abuse, and acting on reports.
  • To understand what’s working and improve the product.

We do not sell your personal data, and we don’t show third-party ads.

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in members can see your profile, your forum posts and ideas, events you host, and messages you send in city chat.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you’re talking to in the app. They are stored in encrypted form, and we don’t read them except where required by law or to investigate a serious safety report.
  • Public events: if you tick “Make this event public” when hosting, anyone — including search engines — can see that event’s title, description, date, time, venue, city, your first name and how many people are going, at its own link. Who is going and online meeting links stay inside the app. Events are members-only unless you choose this.
  • Clarity check-ins are private to you.
  • Your email address is not shown to other members.

Services we rely on

We use trusted providers to run The Stillroom. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database, photo storage and analytics.
  • Vercel — website hosting.

These providers may store data on servers outside India.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences, and Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits. You can clear these in your browser settings at any time; you’ll be signed out.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Messages you sent in group spaces may be removed or shown without your name.

Your rights

You can:

  • See and correct most of your profile yourself in Edit profile.
  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data, or delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Raise a complaint with us, and if you’re not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To do any of these, email support@thestillroom.site from the address linked to your account.

Age

The Stillroom is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has signed up, tell us and we’ll remove the account.

Security

We use access rules, encryption in transit (HTTPS) and the security of our providers to protect your data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. For significant changes, we’ll let members know in the app.

Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, data requests or complaints, email support@thestillroom.site. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.
