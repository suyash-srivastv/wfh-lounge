The Stillroom works in every city in India — from Bangalore and Mumbai to Prayagraj, Varanasi and Kanpur.

How it works

  • Set your home city when you join. Type any city or town.
  • See who’s nearby: members who live and work in your city.
  • City chat: one shared room for everyone in your city.
  • Events: join or host small meetups — coffee, walks, co-working, board games.

New to your city on The Stillroom?

Every city starts small. Say hello in the city chat, invite a few friends or colleagues, and host the first meetup. It only takes a handful of people to get a city going.
